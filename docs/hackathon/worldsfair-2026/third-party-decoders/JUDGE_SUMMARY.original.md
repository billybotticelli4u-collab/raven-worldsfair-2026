# Judge-facing summary (one paragraph)

We ran Raven's 30-vector Solana transaction-version corpus (corpus 1.4, sha256 7e7425a9…802c, repo pin 2cb12875) through thin adapters around unmodified published decoders. @solana/kit 8.4.0, 8.3.0, 4.0.0 and 3.0.3 and @solana/web3.js 1.99.0 (`VersionedTransaction.deserialize`) ran in Raven's official sandboxed runner, unchanged, with each adapter bundled into one file. solders 0.29.0 ran in a separate small harness outside the runner. Raw matches: kit 8.4.0 16/30, kit 8.3.0 16/30, kit 4.0.0 24/30, kit 3.0.3 23/30, web3.js 17/30, solders 15/30. These numbers are not a quality ranking. Three rows test JSON and base64 input handling, which our adapter does, not the decoder. Fourteen rows test sanitize and policy rules (size and count caps, duplicate accounts, header consistency, heap bounds) that these libraries do not claim to enforce. Older kit versions score higher only because they reject every v1 transaction. On the 13 structural rows (version detection, envelope, truncation, v1 layout, exact byte consumption) the results are kit 8.4.0 11/13, kit 8.3.0 11/13, kit 4.0.0 9/13, kit 3.0.3 8/13, web3.js 12/13 and solders 11/13. Every structural divergence has one of three causes: no v1 support in kit 4.0.0 and earlier; a version check that kit fixed in 4.0.0; or trailing-byte tolerance that the kit maintainers recorded as won't-fix (issue #1963). One follow-up item came out of this. A standalone script with no Raven code shows that kit 3.0.3 through 8.4.0 and web3.js 1.99.0 accept non-minimal compact-u16 lengths (e.g. `82 00` for 2), which solders' Agave-derived short-vec rejects. We found no existing issue for it, but we have not assessed its impact. The corpus showed this only indirectly, at V10 on older versions; no current vector isolates it. Both of Raven's valid v1 vectors were made with kit 8.3.0. Two independent decoders, web3.js 1.99.0 and solders 0.29.0, also accept them. That supports, but does not prove, Raven's reading of the v1 layout, and SIMD-0385 is still in review.
