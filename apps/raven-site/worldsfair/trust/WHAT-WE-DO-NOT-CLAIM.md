# What this Raven demonstration does not claim

- A passing corpus proves only the tested expectations for that target, profile and corpus. It does not prove complete conformance, general safety or absence of bugs.
- A disagreement is not automatically an exploit, vulnerability, malicious behavior or unsafe token.
- Raven-owned demonstration targets are not independent customers or third-party defect discoveries.
- This Fair candidate does not establish Solana chain parity, deployed wallet behavior, x402 behavior or external-library profile conclusions.
- Recorded results are historical measurements. A recorded walkthrough is not a fresh test of the current public website.
- Node permission flags do not provide network denial or a complete malicious-code sandbox. Local platform results do not prove behavior on another host.
- A supplied checksum establishes equality with expected bytes, not who produced them or whether the expectation is trustworthy. A displayed commit and UI fingerprint are not full deployment attestation.
- A review GO is bounded to its stated artifact and scope. It is not Owner acceptance or authority to deploy, publish or change production settings.
- Foundry, Corpus0, the external verifier pilot, ACP and Pulse remain separate unless an exact integration is explicitly built and reviewed. INTEGER_KEY_ORDER remains unresolved.
- This candidate does not offer arbitrary public code upload, accounts, billing, a certification authority or a public multi-tenant verifier service.
