RAVEN. / evidence

Evidence source registry

What Raven checks, what each source can and cannot prove, and what happens when a source is unavailable. Machine-readable: /evidence-sources.json.

Required: token mint account (authorities, supply, full Token-2022 extension tail) and the caller-supplied token program. Optional: metadata account, Raydium CPMM pool (LP custody + registered vaults), holder accounts (key-gated beta, venue-adjusted only with decoded evidence). Plus the attestation public key and the stamped engine version.

Excluded by design: social sentiment, price prediction, future-performance inference, opaque third-party rug scores.

Failure behavior: Raven fails closed — required evidence missing means unknowable, never a default pass; optional evidence not supplied or not checkable becomes an explicit coverage gap.
